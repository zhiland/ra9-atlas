## Three targeted readings and their limits

1. **The Hostage — local branch.** PowerPyx describes clue search and negotiations with Daniel, including the choice to help the injured police officer (obey or disobey) and conditions for trust/endings. The text explicitly notes a cross-chapter consequence of saving the officer for *Public Enemy*. The guide text was checked, but not the full flowchart itself. The page refers to user tips in comments and includes variants/clarifications, so disputed exact sequences need independent confirmation.
2. **A New Home → later Kara state.** In *A New Home*, the text says friendly replies to Alice give Kara a key, which lets her open the box and inspect its contents. Examining the gun in Todd’s nightstand is separately marked as affecting a later chapter. The reviewed excerpt from the *Battle for Detroit — Kara* page confirms that the meeting with Todd is available only if he did not die in *Stormy Night*. No evidence was found that the Alice box itself changes the *Battle for Detroit* branch; do not infer that connection. The causal link “box → later scene” needs checking against an in-game flowchart or other independent documentation.
3. **Crossroads — complex Connor branching.** The checked Connor page says outcomes of earlier chapters affect available routes; whether Markus is alive changes the person Connor speaks to in the captain’s cabin, and high Software Instability opens the choice to become deviant. The page links that choice to a separate Connor storyline in the finale. Only Connor’s flowchart was checked, not Kara’s, Markus’s, or all nodes; the claimed 100% coverage and exact thresholds need independent review.
