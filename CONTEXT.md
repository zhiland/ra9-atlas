# rA9 Atlas language

The atlas describes sourced game flowcharts and the limits of that evidence.

## Flowchart interpretation

**Row group**:
A visibly bracketed set of labeled alternatives in a source flowchart. Its rows
can describe activities, results or variations, without proving direct player choice.
_Avoid_: player decision, unless separate evidence establishes that meaning.

**Common group exit**:
A continuous connector that leaves the outer bracket of a row group. It does not
by itself prove a separate transition from every row or simultaneous completion.
_Avoid_: every outcome unlocks the next step, unless separately supported.

**Traced connector**:
A continuous source-chart line between identified boxes, rows or brackets. It
records visible structure, without proving gameplay rules or availability.
_Avoid_: verified game transition.
