# Source register

Snapshot: 2026-10-18; Stormy Night escape and entry sources plus the A New Home second-floor slice added 2026-10-20; Stage 1 Hostage/Public Enemy pilot sources added 2026-10-21. This register records how sources have informed the prototype and preparation work. “Verified” below means inspected for the stated scope; it does not mean checked against a controlled playthrough or the complete game. Site claims of completeness remain claims. Public access does not grant permission to reproduce game text, screenshots, or third-party material. Per-source reuse rights were audited on 2026-10-18: see the [source license audit](notes/source-license-audit.md) for each source's rights status, evidence, and recommended action.

## Game and flowchart evidence

The 2026-10-20 content expansion also consulted the full [PowerPyx *A New Home* guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-a-new-home-walkthrough-100/) for the optional nightstand-gun check and opened-window action, the [PowerPyx *Stormy Night* guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-stormy-night-walkthrough-100/) for the gun confrontation and distinct shooting outcomes, and the [PowerPyx Kara finale guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-battle-for-detroit-kara-walkthrough-100/) plus [Gamer Guides Kara finale guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/detroit-become-human/guide/walkthrough/chapter-1/chapter-31-battle-for-detroit-kara) for the checkpoint detour. These are secondary sources; independence is unknown, the exact 11-minute boundary remains unresolved, and no transition was checked in game. Only concise paraphrases and source links are used.

A further 2026-10-20 Stormy Night slice used PowerPyx, Gamer Guides and GameFAQs to represent two *successful non-gun* routes: reach Alice → Run Away → hide until Todd passes → front-door/backyard exit, and lock Alice’s room → use the window opened in *A New Home* → choose Go out → pass the reported QTE → take Alice down to the bus. The guides describe the routes but do **not** state Todd’s later state for either one. The links from both escape outcomes to the existing “Todd survives” state are therefore labelled inferences, not traced links, and one explicit unknown record holds that gap. See the [Stormy Night escape slice](notes/stormy-escape-slice.md).

A final 2026-10-20 entry slice used the PowerPyx “Todd Gets Mad / Becoming Deviant or not” section and Gamer Guides Branches 1–3 to represent Todd ordering Kara to stay still, Alice running upstairs, and the response branches. Staying still leads only to the terminal “Todd kills Alice and Kara” outcome, distinct from “Todd killed”. Becoming deviant branches to go upstairs (joining the existing Reach Alice route) or Reason with Todd, which stops where Todd knocks Kara down and reaches Alice before Kara. Both guides are secondary, independence is unknown, and no transition was checked in game. See the [Stormy Night entry slice](notes/stormy-entry-slice.md).

A 2026-10-20 A New Home second-floor slice used the same PowerPyx and GameFAQs/Mogg guides. PowerPyx names the two Todd-room magazine titles; GameFAQs reports only one unnamed magazine, so the exact titles stay PowerPyx-only. GameFAQs places the pill bottle before the bedside-table handgun; PowerPyx reports checking the nightstand directly. Both place Alice going to her room after Todd’s bed. Their other second-floor orders differ, so the map adds only the pills→gun and bed→Alice-room sequences and records the rest as unknown. GameFAQs says Alice trusts Kara enough to give the key but names no relationship level; PowerPyx gives no threshold either. See the [second-floor slice](notes/anewhome-secondfloor-slice.md).

The 2026-10-21 Stage 1 pilot transcribed all visible structure from the Steam *The Hostage* chart and used PowerPyx *The Hostage* closing-choice section and *Public Enemy* for the saved-cop carry. The Hostage guide reports that saving the wounded cop makes him appear later; the Public Enemy guide names the “Meet Cop From Hostage Situation” interaction under a saved-during-The-Hostage state. Only the incoming prerequisite and that meeting node are drawn. Later cop rescue/kill outcomes, the exact state check, and everything after the meeting are outside the slice. The executor’s GameFAQs/Mogg *Public Enemy* request returned HTTP 403. A separate reviewer accessed the page on 2026-10-21 and confirmed its §192 report that the officer Connor saved in the first chapter later stops him and thanks him. Record these as separate access outcomes; neither guide agreement nor reviewer access verifies the game transition or proves independent guide research. See the [Hostage chart ledger](notes/hostage-poc3-coverage.md).

The PoC4 Hostage correction uses a Shazoo article that embeds a secondary-hosted screenshot of the in-game chart at 48% completion. The reviewer inspected the image on 2026-10-21 and used only the selected blue path to distinguish the option-specific Cold Approach → FAIL TO NEGOTIATE connector and the visible FAIL TO NEGOTIATE → DEVIANT JUMPS WITH EMMA connector. This is chart-image corroboration, not a new full-chart inventory, proof of Friendly’s outcome, or gameplay verification. The project includes no screenshot or copied article text; reuse permission was not identified. The old index-89 grouped candidate remains uncertain but is narrowed to Friendly; the Cold candidate is a separate internal edge claim, so the source chart remains at 36 pictured connectors while the atlas has 37 claims. See the [Hostage chart ledger](notes/hostage-poc3-coverage.md) and [source license audit](notes/source-license-audit.md).

## Reading the project’s evidence labels

- **Guide-reported** means a secondary guide states the fact; matching guides can still share a source.
- **Transcript text** supports wording or scene details, not hidden game logic.
- **Screenshot/OCR** supports only what is legible and visible in that image.
- **In-game verified** requires direct observation in the game under recorded conditions. No transition in the current prototype has this status.
