# Check of examples D1 and D3

Date: October 17, 2026. This compares published guides; it is not an in-game check. The platform and game version for the described transitions are unknown. No direct gameplay capture or complete verified video was available below.

## D1 — The Hostage: find gun → keep it → use it

- **Guide-supported sequence:** reconstruct the police officer’s murder at the dining table, turn the view toward the kitchen table, and pick up the gun. Then, when Daniel asks whether Connor is armed, lie to keep the gun; only then does the final action Use Gun appear. PowerPyx, *The Hostage*, “Search for Clues,” “Negotiate with Deviant,” and “Endings”: [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-the-hostage-opening-walkthrough-100/).
- **Keep states distinct:** `found/picked up` is one node; `kept after the question` is a separate choice/state; `Use Gun` is a conditional action at the end. Finding the gun alone is not enough to shoot: if Connor tells the truth, he drops it. If he picks it up and lies, Use Gun opens the next choice. `Shoot` leads to an ending where Connor shoots Daniel; PowerPyx describes the sequence `Intimidate > Convince` leading to an ending where Daniel shoots Connor. Do not collapse the intermediate Use Gun choice into an automatic ending.
- **Cross-check:** Game of Guides, *The Hostage*, “Investigate Cop’s Body” and “Ending Choice 5–6,” [section](https://video-game-guide-walkthrough.supersoluce.com/solution/detroit-become-human-guide-walkthrough/the-hostage/), independently confirms on its site and in its own presentation the gun pickup, lie, keeping the gun, and two different endings. This is also a written guide, not gameplay footage; independence of the underlying observations has not been established.
- **Counterexample / branch:** find the gun but leave it (PowerPyx, “Leave Cop’s Gun”), or pick it up and tell the truth (“Be honest about Gun”); in the latter case the gun is dropped and the final `Use Gun` is unavailable. A model with one “picked up gun” flag or a direct `picked up → shot` link is therefore inaccurate.
- **D1 verdict: GO for the data model** if it stores separate actions, prerequisites, and outcomes. **HOLD for an “checked in game” label** until an in-game playthrough or direct video evidence is available.

## D3 — Battle for Detroit (Kara): bypass checkpoint → bus

- **Guide-supported conditional link:** in “Kara Leaving Detroit,” at the checkpoint choice, a detour that costs two minutes with less than 11 minutes on the timer leads to `Delayed / Late for the Bus / Last Bus departed`. Taking the detour immediately while the timer is above 11 minutes leads to `At the Bus Terminal`. The source does not establish what happens at exactly 11:00. PowerPyx, “Security Checkpoint,” [section](https://www.powerpyx.com/detroit-become-human-battle-for-detroit-kara-walkthrough-100/).
- **Cross-check:** Gamer Guides, *Chapter 31 – Battle for Detroit (Kara)*, “Kara Leaving Detroit,” [section](https://www.gamerguides.com/detroit-become-human/guide/walkthrough/chapter-1/chapter-31-battle-for-detroit-kara), gives the same threshold and both branches; the page names an author and PC/PS4. A Neoseeker search snippet also says that when the detour is taken with less than 11 minutes, soldiers announce that the bus has left: [Battle for Detroit Kara](https://www.neoseeker.com/detroit-become-human/walkthrough/Battle_for_Detroit_Kara) (the page did not open for line-by-line review). Gamer Guides’ text closely matches PowerPyx; independence from a shared source is unproven. Neoseeker supports only the negative branch.
- **Positive and negative cases:** more than 11 minutes + immediate detour — reach the terminal/on time; less than 11 minutes after waiting — late/the last bus has left. Thus, both guides’ positive case refutes the claim `detour always causes lateness`. The condition is time remaining when choosing, not merely the fact of taking a detour.
- **Unresolved discrepancy:** Supersoluce, “Security Checkpoint,” [“Main Path – Security Checkpoint”](https://video-game-guide-walkthrough.supersoluce.com/solution/detroit-become-human-guide-walkthrough/battle-for-detroit/battle-for-detroit-kara-leaving-detroit/), lists `Delayed / Late for the Bus / Last Bus Departs` branches, but then says both paths do not change the rest of the chapter and does not describe a timing condition. This may be an omission in the description, not a refutation of the threshold.
- **D3 verdict: GO for a conditional model** that stores the timer/prerequisite, detour choice, and later branch; **HOLD for in-game confirmation**. Both timer cases need checking in one known game build.
